# Partner Performance Scorer: 100-word summary

Partner managers do not trust black-box scores and finance cannot audit them, so I split the job in two. A deterministic scorecard rates every merchant, fleet partner and brand partner on six inputs a Careem partner team already tracks: GMV attainment, fulfilment, repeat rate, on-time rate, tools adopted and engagement. Weights are sliders, so the model is a tool, not a verdict. Score and quarter-on-quarter trend place each partner in Invest, Protect, Fix or Exit. AI only writes three improvement actions tied to the weakest metrics and a business-review brief. Thirty self-generated partners across UAE, KSA, Egypt and Pakistan.
